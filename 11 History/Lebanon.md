
1500 - Othman Empire Rule 

1920 - French Rule following the disintegration of the Othman Empire after WW1, Lebanon strived under French rule 

1932 - A census was made which concluded Christians are the majority, this is important to understand the national pact 

1943- French rule ended because Germany defeated France in WW2 so Lebanon took it’s independence 

1943- Around this time the national pact was made, this pact declares that the president should be  a Maronite Christian, the prime minister a Sunni Muslim and, the speaker of parliament a Shia Muslim 

1948 - Lebanon sided with the Palestinians against the new state of Israel, they were defeated which caused many Palestinians to flee into Lebanon 

1952 - Camille Chamoun became president, he sided with the US which upset Syria and Egypt, however the US helped Lebanon, it’s important ally in the region, to narrowly avoid a civil war at that time 

1958 - Fouad Chehab became president, he didn’t fully side with the US which balanced the country for a while and Lebanon was striving again at this time making it the financial capital of the region 

1958 - Tensions between Christians and Muslims destabilized the country 

Palestinians groups in Lebanon led by Yasser Arafat were always planning attacks on Israel 

1970 - Jordan expelled the Palestinian Liberation Organization (PLO) from its land into Lebanon, further destabilizing the country 

Tensions now grew between Christian groups and Palestinian groups 

1975 - The Palestinians tried to assassinate the Kataeb party leader Pierre Gemayel

The war started between Christians and Palestinians/Muslims

1976 - Syria under the rule of Hafez Al Assad invaded Lebanon to restore order and prevent a Palestinian/Muslim victory which many feared would cause an Israeli invasion 

During this time the Palestinian groups attacked Israel continuously

1982 - Bachir Gemayel became president, he sided with the Israelis to restore balance in Lebanon, he was assassinated by the Syrians before taking office 

1982 - Israel invaded Lebanon and sided with the Christian groups, this is when Christians/Israelis went into two of the biggest Palestinian refugee camps in Beirut and slaughtered over 3000 people 

The Israeli invasion forced the PLO to retreat massively from Lebanon, this is where Iran saw the opportunity to take down its enemy Israel so it send troops to Lebanon and created Hezbollah

Syria shifted from being against the Palestinians/Muslims to becoming with them in order to balance things out, Syrian just didn’t want anyone to have more influence on Lebanon than itself 

Groups realized no one could win this war, and Christian groups who were the strongest now started becoming weak 

International attention grew and counties started to try to resolve the Lebanese war 

1989 - The national reconciliation accord referred to as the Taeif Agreement was signed, this accord was brokered by Saudi Arabia and supported by the US and Syria and focused on balancing out the government between Christians and Muslims

The Taeif Agreement also permitted Syrian forces to remain in the country for a transitional period

All parties surrendered their weapons but Hezbollah didn’t, claiming they were protecting the country from Israel 

1991 - A law was passed which pardons all political crimes committed in the war 

2000 - Israeli troops withdrew from Lebanon as the was ended, however Hezbollah still wanted to fight them as a support for the Palestinian cause 

2005 - Syrian troops withdrew from Lebanon because of an outrage from the Lebanese after the Assassination of the prime minister Rafik Hariri as Syria is believed to be behind this assassination 

2006- Israel responded to Hezbollah by starting a new war with Lebanon
